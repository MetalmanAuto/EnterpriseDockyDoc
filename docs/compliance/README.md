## Roles in one paragraph

For account data (names, emails, phone numbers, plan and payment records) Excelleta decides the purpose and means, so it is the data fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023 and the controller under GDPR and UK GDPR. For the documents customers upload, the customer decides, so the customer is the fiduciary or controller and Excelleta is the processor, working under the DPA. Every provider that touches the data is a sub-processor and must be under a written agreement.
